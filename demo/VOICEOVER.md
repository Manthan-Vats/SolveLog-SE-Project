# SolveLog Demo: Voiceover Script

Video: `SolveLog_Demo.mp4` (2:49, 1440×900, no audio). Read each block when its timestamp
comes up. Each block fits its time slot at a relaxed pace (about 2–2.5 words per second).
If you run long, pause the video in your editor, or slow that segment down; nothing on screen
is time-critical.

---

**0:00 – 0:18 · Landing page**
> This is SolveLog, a practice journal for competitive programmers. It keeps every problem you
> solve in one place, reminds you to revise it before you forget, and turns your work into a
> public portfolio.

**0:18 – 0:23 · Sign-in**
> Access is invite-only. Visitors request an invite, and the operator approves them.

**0:23 – 0:36 · Dashboard**
> After signing in you land on the dashboard. It shows a setup checklist, your current streak,
> how many problems are due for revision today, and your most recent entries.

**0:36 – 0:54 · Problems journal**
> The journal holds every problem I've logged, here forty-nine of them, from LeetCode and
> Codeforces. I can search, and filter by platform, rating, tag or language. Each card shows the
> difficulty, the time I took, and the topic tags.

**0:54 – 1:12 · Problem detail**
> Opening a problem shows its statement, my saved solution with syntax highlighting, and a solve
> timer that records every attempt. Each solution can be analysed by AI, which tags the
> algorithms and data structures I used.

**1:12 – 1:35 · Add problem**
> Adding a problem takes one link. I paste a Codeforces URL and click fetch. SolveLog pulls the
> title, platform, difficulty and tags automatically. Then I can paste my code and file it.
> The browser extension can do this in one click, straight from the judge's page.

**1:35 – 1:55 · Analytics**
> Analytics shows a year-long activity heatmap, my solved count and streaks, which topics I
> practise most, and my average solve time per topic. That's how I spot my weak areas.

**1:55 – 2:09 · Revision**
> Revision uses the FSRS spaced-repetition algorithm. Each card asks me to recall the approach.
> I reveal my solution and grade myself, and FSRS schedules the next review at the right time.

**2:09 – 2:30 · Settings**
> In settings I set my public handle, verify my judge accounts, add my own Gemini API key for
> the AI features (it's encrypted at rest), and manage the tokens used by the browser extension.

**2:30 – 2:49 · Public portfolio**
> Finally, the public portfolio. This is the one link I can share with recruiters: only the
> problems I choose to publish, with my activity and stats. Everything else stays private by
> default. That's SolveLog. Thank you.

---

### Recording tips

- Record the audio in one take with any phone or headset mic, then line it up in an editor
  (Clipchamp is built into Windows 11; CapCut and DaVinci Resolve are free).
- In Clipchamp: drag in `SolveLog_Demo.mp4` and your audio file, line them up, then Export at 1080p.
